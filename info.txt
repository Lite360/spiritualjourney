SPIRITUAL JOURNEY
Full Product Development Prompt

Build a modern, elegant, responsive Christian digital-content platform called Spiritual Journey.

Spiritual Journey is a personal Christian media and reflection brand founded by Ife Dayo. The platform exists to share God's Word, biblical reflections, teachings, stories, devotionals, audio messages, videos, resources, and upcoming programs that help people grow spiritually, discover purpose, understand their identity, and deepen their relationship with God.

The website must feel like a modern Christian editorial platform, not a traditional church website.

The design should be simple, warm, premium, calm, authentic, and highly readable.
